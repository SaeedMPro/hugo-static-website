Computer Networks Research Group · Bu-Ali Sina University

Networks that manage themselves, defend themselves and use less energy.

MCTN is the computer networks research group of the Department of Computer Engineering at Bu-Ali Sina University. We work on software-defined networking, IoT and sensor networks, network security and traffic engineering.
