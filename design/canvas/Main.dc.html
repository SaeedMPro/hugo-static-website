Layout

Content column 1200 max, side gutters 16–40. Sections breathe 96 apart on desktop, 64 on phones. Cards sit on the ground with a 1 px line, no shadow; one night band per page at most.

Motif — links and nodes

Research areas are nodes on one network. The amber packet is the only moving thing on the site; it stops for visitors who prefer reduced motion.
